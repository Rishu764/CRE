### Vision
CRE raises people-centric nationally important issues on environment, agriculture, planning process, eco-tourism, tribal and rural development, and a wide gamut of policy issues on governance, nationalism, and livelihood — writing regular columns in national newspapers and taking up matters with concerned government and other authorities.

### Mission
To work at the grassroots level for environmental conservation, sustainable forest management, tribal welfare, rural livelihood enhancement, and climate-resilient development — without dependence on external grants.

### Core Values
- People-centric approach
- Self-sustained operations (funded through personal resources)
- Grassroots engagement
- Evidence-based advocacy
- South-South cooperation
- Traditional knowledge integration

## 🔄 HOW WE WORK

### Our Approach
1. **Identify Issues:** Visit grassroots communities, understand problems firsthand
2. **Build Networks:** Create confederations of NGOs and community organizations
3. **Advocate:** Write in national media, approach government authorities, file with tribunals
4. **Train & Empower:** Conduct training programmes, form SHGs, build capacity
5. **Follow Through:** Monitor implementation, maintain pressure, ensure outcomes

### Geographic Reach
- **Uttarakhand:** Dehradun, Almora, Haridwar, Pauri, Tehri, Nainital

2. Technology development and knowledge sharing among developing nations
3. Return to traditional roots to combat climate change
4. Climate-resilient development administration at district/sub-district levels
5. Top priority on water rejuvenation and aquifer recharge
6. Forest fire and disaster control
7. Collaborative research with multi-lateral and bi-lateral funding
